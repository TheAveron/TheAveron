## 🌌 Featured Projects

### 🛰️ [TIPE-LAGRANGE](https://github.com/TheAveron/TIPE-LAGRANGE): Station-keeping of the JWST around L2
*My most advanced project.* A from-scratch simulation of the James Webb Space Telescope's halo orbit around the Sun–Earth L2 point, and of the corrections needed to keep it there.
Circular restricted three-body problem, RK4 integrator, halo orbit construction (Richardson + differential correction), monodromy matrix and eigenvector-based station-keeping. Corrections keep the position error at a few hundred km, versus up to 10⁸ km without them.
`Python` `NumPy` `SciPy` `Matplotlib`
 
### 🌍 [ISS Vision](https://github.com/TheAveron/ISS-Vision): live ISS tracker
Final project of Harvard's CS50. A Flask web app that shows the ISS's current position and future trajectory from orbital (TLE) data, plus crew, speed and altitude, and a calculator for the next three passes over your location. Users can create an account to save their map settings.
`Python` `Flask` `SQLite` `JavaScript`
 
### 🪐 [Wikispace](https://github.com/TheAveron/Wikispace): a wiki of the solar system
A space-themed website about the planets of the solar system, written in French. My most finished and polished web project. [Live demo](https://theaveron.github.io/Wikispace/)
`HTML` `CSS`
 
### ☄️ [Whack-an-Asteroid](https://github.com/TheAveron/Whack-an-Asteroid): a small space game
A whack-a-mole with a twist: defend Earth from asteroids, avoid hitting the spaceships, and reach level 10 to win. Built for fun.
